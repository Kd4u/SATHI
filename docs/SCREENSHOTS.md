# Screenshot contributions

Useful views: Today (iPhone and Android), iPad notebook, Mac sidebar/calendar, attendance progress, and a populated widget. Light and dark examples are welcome.

Use synthetic classes, names and notes. Hide email addresses, notifications, personal wallpaper, device IDs and college/private records. Do not include Firebase/Google Cloud consoles, signing settings or account exports.

Capture the actual current app, state the platform/version, and keep mockups labelled as concepts. Submit images in `docs/assets/screenshots/` with a small caption and alt text in the README. Aim for readable text and consistent crops rather than large marketing images.
